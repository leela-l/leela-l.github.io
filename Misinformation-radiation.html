Fake 5G News!!

Social media is full of alarming claims about 5G. Below, explore examples and uncover what the science actually says.

It is an important skill to be able to spot these for yourself. See here for information about how to spot misconceptions in physics.

Fake 5G Tower Warning
INSERT IMAGE

This poster was shared widely on facebook and instagram. It displays a fake warning on a 5G tower, not an official one.

Claim: 5G towers are damaging to humans and animals, and can cause severe health issues.

Reality: There is no evidence that any 5G technology is harmful to humans. The frequencies 20KHz to 6GHz used by 5G cell towers are non-ionising, meaning they do not mutate or affect DNA. Some studies quote that 5G uses frequencies up to 300GHz, however this is the theoretical upper bound, used as a safety limit for future research. In reality the maximum frequencies 5G uses is no greater than in the 10s of GHz at its peak. See here for more information on the specific frequencies used by 5G.

Fake RF Meter and 5G Health Warnings
INSERT VIDEO

This video shows an RF meter, a device used to measure electromagnetic field strength, measuring the field next to a 5G tower. It appears to be increasing out of control, the video implying that the electromagnetic field here is at an extreme high. In reality, RF meters can be highly inaccurate. Consumer level meters will average out all nearby frequencies rather than showing ones explicitly produced by the 5G tower, thus producing a much higher record than expected. The man-made electromagnetic radiation is much less than usual naturally caused radiation. RF meters are also often miscalibrated, meaning that it is adjusted incorrectly to the environment, possibly leading to inaccurately high results. This reading is shown in the video without any comparison to a safety limit, so it is unclear by how much the radiation has truly increased.

This display of information without true context or fact has led many people to believe the frequencies produced by these towers are threats to human life, as shown by the list of health issues in the video that it supposedly causes. Again like the last image, no evidence supports this claim.

Fake Scientific Studies on 5G

Consider this website https://www.naturalnews.com/2024-01-11-4-ways-5g-technology-can-harm-humans.html?

It claims that 5G has scientific links to premature ageing, early onset Alzheimer's, brain damage, and cancer. These claims made with scientific ‘facts’ are extreme extrapolations of the truth, with facts being taken completely out of context. Take their claim that millimetre waves alter DNA, for example. It is true that studies have shown that some high frequency millimetre waves do indeed mutate DNA, however, these studies implement electromagnetic waves of a much higher frequency than is ever used for 5G, wifi and bluetooth. The papers that this article references are controversial, are not heavily peer reviewed, and disagree with scientific consensus across the globe, thus are showing a misrepresentation of the truth.

Though there is a lack of scientific evidence explicitly concluding that 5G frequencies are not harmful to humans, it does not automatically mean that it is a danger. Perfect studies on people cannot be done due to ethical limitations. Etc.
