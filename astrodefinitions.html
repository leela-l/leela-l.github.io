Sucked In - Pseudoscience in Blackholes

Definitions

Black Hole
A region of space where the gravitational field is so strong, not even light can escape.
Collapse
The process by which a star will begin to fall in on itself due to gravity when it runs out of fuel to burn.
Supernova
A star that suddenly increases greatly in brightness due to an explosion following its collapse that expells matter outward.
CMB (Cosmic Microwave Background)
Microwave radiation that uniformly fills space in the Universe. It is a remenant from the early universe.
Event Horizon
The boundry of o blackhole, beyond which no light or other radiation can escape.
Schwarzschild Radius
The physical radius of the event horizon
Singularity
An infinitessimally small point at the centre of a blackhole where density is said to be infinite.
Wormhole
A hypothetical connection between two distant regions of spacetime.
Einstein–Rosen Bridge
A type of wormhole proposed by Albert Einstein and Nathan Rosen.
Space-time Curvature
The bending of spacetime caused by massive objects. This effect is what we percieve as gravity.
Torsion
A property of spacetime in general relativity where, in addition to curvature, spacetime can 'twist'.
Information Paradox
Quantum mechanics dictates that information must be conserved. However, a contradiction arises when it is considered that information must be destroyed if an object eneters a blackhole.
Time Dilation
The phenomenon where time passes slower for an object moving or in a stronger gravitational field relative to an observer.
LIGO
Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory. A US-based physics project designed to detect gravitational waves from space.
