5G: Is it a death wish?

You may have heard the term radiation when talking about sunrays, microwaves, X-rays, or wireless networks, but what is actually radiation? Are all of these dangerous cancer causing rays?

There has been widespread panic about the health risks of radiation over social media and the news, so much so that it has been seen to influence politics. The most significant example is 5G, which we will discuss and debunk below.

The reason for the controversy is a large misspread of information about the harm radiation can cause. Posts quote scientific papers without properly understanding it, showing only snapshots of scientific knowledge. This portrays the health risks to be much greater than it really is. See here to see examples of this fake news.

Here, we are going to mythbust the rumours about radiation, but first let's test your knowledge.
