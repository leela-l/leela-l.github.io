Many people find the use of radiation controversial, particularly in relation to wireless networks. The most significant example is 5G, which we will discuss and debunk below. There is a large misspread of information about the harm radiation can cause and this is due the general public’s lack of understanding of this topic. This had led to widespread panic about its health risks over social media, news, and even influencing politics. Posts on social media can lead users down ‘rabbit holes’, feeding them an endless supply of the same misinformation, making users believe it is indeed true. News articles and posts quote scientific papers without properly understanding it, showing only snapshots of scientific knowledge, portraying the health risks to be much greater than in reality.

Lets analyse and break down the facts from the fiction in some of these headlines and posts about radiation.
