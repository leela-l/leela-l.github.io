Spotting Misinformation!

When scrolling on social media and the internet, it is easy to believe everything in front of your eyes. But we now know it is easy to fabricate information, or to emphasise certain stories without explaining the whole context. As shown on this website, it can even lead to global panic, such as believing that 5G caused the spread of Covid 19, or that climate change is a hoax.

Let's talk a bit about how you can spot these misconceptions so you don't fall victim to one of these beliefs

Top Tips for Spotting Misinformation:

Check the Sources

  • Are they from a reputable website?
  • Has the website been cited by other websites?
  • Are the scientific papers it references peer reviewed?
  • Does it back up claims with empirical evidence?

Bias

  • Does the website have any political affiliations or views that might impact how they present information?
  • Read multiple sources to see if they have the same information.
  • Does the website have financial motivation to report certain information?

Check the Writing

  • Is it well written?
  • Does it use sensational language to exaggerate claims?
  • Does it confuse speculation with fact?

ALWAYS BE SKEPTICAL!
