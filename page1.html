Common Misunderstandings

1. Climate has always changed

It is true climate has changed a lot over the Earth’s 4.5-billion-year history. There is a natural cycle of warm and cold periods that happen over hundreds of thousands of years. But when people talk about climate change, they mean human-made climate change. Human activity such as burning fossil fuels, cutting down trees, and farming livestock are contributing to the Earth’s rising temperature. Changes that would normally happen over thousands of years are happening in decades. The last eleven years stand out as the eleven warmest to be directly observed. This rapid warming cannot be explained by natural cycles. Multiple studies have found roughly 97% of climate scientists agree that humans are responsible for climate change.
