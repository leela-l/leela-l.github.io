Where does Quantum Physics come from?

Quantum Physics as a framework arose after existing classical theories, like Maxwell’s equations, could not explain things at very small sizes, like the emission of light from heated metals, or the properties of crystals.

However, several paradigmatic shifts, like that of Max Planck, which involved treating energy like discrete energy packets (quanta), resolved these descrepancies.

Specifically, he said that the frequency of a photon (light as particles) is proportional to its energy.

The resulting consequence would lay the foundation of quantum mechanics. Building on this initial postulate, things like the uncertainty principle and the wavefunction would arise out of the mathematics, and were subsequently confirmed in laboratory environments.

Misuse of fundamental concepts in Quantum Physics

Emotional wellbeing and "good vibes": proponents of "quantum healing" say you must understand yourself and your feelings, in terms of vibrations- any negativity is due to not resonating at the correct frequencies. [A]

Healing Crystals: Whilst quite rarely talked about in this regard, when mentioned, the idea of vibrations inside crystals are misused- or equally the crystals can 'absorb energy', or their magnetic properties can deflect bad energy. [B].
