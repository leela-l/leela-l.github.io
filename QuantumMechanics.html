Where does Quantum Physics come from?

Quantum mechanics emerged when classical physics failed to explain microscopic phenomena such as blackbody radiation and atomic spectra.

Max Planck proposed that energy is emitted in discrete packets called quanta, fundamentally changing physics.

Later developments introduced wavefunctions, probability, and the uncertainty principle, showing that nature at small scales is inherently probabilistic rather than deterministic.
