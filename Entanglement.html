What is Quantum Entanglment?

Entanglement is by far one of the most "overhyped" concept in all of quantum physics.

The following analogy helps to illustrate the notion of entanglement:

First consider the "0th" step, it is merely to ensure the experiment holds by ensuring that the objects are casually related. Here we take a Spin 0 particle (spin is basically the angular momentum of a particle that is fundamental to it) at rest and let it decay: all you have to know is that spin is conserved, so either particle shares exactly half the magnitude, in opposite signs- up and down.

We then package these new particles, and send them to our friends Alice and Bob, who unfortunatley live a great distance apart

Now say thousands of years pass (this detail is not relevant), and Bob's space mail lands on Earth. He writes to let us know about the package, and upon opening discovers his particle is spin up. Alice's space mail also arrives, and she says hers is spin down

But, Bob then scarily remarks that he then knew that Alice must have had spin down- all without ever knowing what she got. But this means that information instantly, would have travelled across the Milky Way, faster than light!

So what is going on?

Bob knows "instantaenously" about Alice's particle spin, not because there is a secret phone-line tethering the two, but because of logical deduction- he would be able to know right off the bat that his spin is equal and opposite to Alice's.

Any mathematical depicition of entagnlement, just refers to this logical relationship

Key nuances of entanglement

  • Measurement is random- one cannot just "pre-coordinate" them in such a way as to form a coherent message
  • Once a measurement is made, entanglement is "broken"
  • Requires very isolated and specific conditions
Misuse of Quantum Entanglement

Telepathy and fortune telling: Abusers claim that using entanglement, minds are interconnected into some hive/collective conciousness, so can 'tap' into it via telepathy.

Remote healing: In the 2010's, QuantumMAN on a website ran by the Zurich-Alpine Group, claimed you could download medicine as opposed to oral or intravenous methods via a "Portal Access Key" which could transmit data into you. [A]
