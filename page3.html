The invention of Ibupro-spin: the misuse of quantum physics in alternative medicine

Since the growing development of the field of quantum physics, a multitude of “medical professionals” and self-help gurus have jumped onboard, proclaiming the secret quantum world that we must tap into in order that we may become prosperous and free from disease.

Yet, their claims are riddled with pseudoscience and ignorance of the actual field.

This article aims to discuss the issue and offer explanations of what quantum physics really is, so that you, the reader, may better realise the errors of quantum quackery.

Quantum Physics is not particularly accessible to most of the public. Here, however, we hope to clarify particular concepts in the form of analogies.

Many concepts are abused. We intend to cover those most frequently misused: the origin of quantum physics, energy, and quantum fields.

De-mystifying Quantum Physics

Though we aim to clarify these ideas, we cannot promise triviality — even using analogies. Quantum mechanics often runs counter to everyday experience.

As Richard Feynman famously said, sometimes the best thing one can do is simply to “shut up and calculate.”

Where does Quantum Physics come from?

Quantum Physics arose when classical theories, such as Maxwell’s equations, could not explain phenomena at very small scales.

A major shift occurred when Max Planck proposed that energy is emitted in discrete packets, called quanta.

He showed that the frequency of light is proportional to its energy.

This laid the foundation for quantum mechanics, including the uncertainty principle and the wavefunction — ideas later confirmed experimentally.

Quantum Fields

Quantum fields are not secret energy reservoirs. They form the backbone of Quantum Field Theory (QFT), developed to describe quantum phenomena at high energies.

QFT is an abstract mathematical framework that makes remarkably accurate predictions, such as the Higgs boson.

Fields are not tangible objects. We measure their consequences — such as particle interactions — not the field itself.

In standard quantum mechanics, the wavefunction solves the Schrödinger equation and describes how a quantum system behaves.

In QFT, fields are operators acting on Fock space — a mathematical structure of possible quantum states. Disturbances in these fields manifest as particles.

The framework predicted new particles, such as the positron, before they were experimentally observed.

Misuse of Quantum Fields

Variants of the “law of attraction” claim that one can manipulate quantum fields to alter destiny.

Emotional wellbeing is sometimes described as being “out of tune” with a universal quantum field, treated as a literal and manipulable substance.

Conclusion

Despite its quirkiness, quantum physics is not mystical — it is mathematics that makes extremely accurate predictions.

There are no quantum fields to harness, nor negative energies to expel. Physics describes nature regardless of interpretation.

From Democritus’ atomos to modern quantum theory, physics is the refinement of our mathematical descriptions of matter — not the discovery of hidden mystical forces.
