Impacts of 5G Misinformation

The impacts of the misinformation surrounding 5G are vast, particularly in politics. People's fears continue to be surrounding health impacts, but also the conspiracy of 5G being used by government agencies for malicious purposes. It implies that 5G is used to ‘spy’ on the public. Particularly in the height of the pandemic, rumours circulated the globe that 5G was the cause of the spread of the coronavirus.

These conspiracies are told as they get more clicks on the internet for promoting controversial and radical news.

It emphasises mistrust in government agencies as they are perceived to be hiding the truth about 5G and wireless technologies. It also puts mistrust in mainstream science and industry methods, framing modern technology as harmful.

Below are just a few key examples of how the misuse of information surrounding 5G has impacted politics, though the impact is worldwide.

Global 5G Misinformation Around the World

Australia
In 2020 the Australian government was pressured by the increase of misinformation surrounding 5G. A strong anti-5G movement powered by social media caused a nationwide backlash against the rollout of 5G, since their small cells would be in a significant number of places, making it difficult to avoid supposed exposure to the radiation. There was a large panic about the proposed higher frequencies used, but as debunked in this article, we know the truth is that these higher frequencies do not equate to higher health risks.
Italy
In 2022, a right-wing electoral list called Vita had a strong anti-5G movement, involving a party called Alleanza Italiana Stop 5G. This appealed to voters who have distrust in government and science, and it formalises these fears and misinformation for political gain.
Belgium
The rollout of 5G was delayed due to a political concern rather than a scientific one. This shows how public health concerns led to political pressure despite being unsupported by scientific evidence. Pressure from anti-5G activists and politicians caused backlash against relaxing the regulations for radiation, despite no scientific evidence concluding that it is harmful.

Switzerland
A report from experts on 5G commissioned by the government did not reach a consensus on the harm that 5G can cause. Though it was concluded to not be proven harmful, the panel could not agree on precise exposure limits. This lack of clarity led to public panic as many people incorrectly linked a general lack of conclusive evidence to harm. As a result, decisions surrounding regulations were up for political debate and public pressure rather than scientific evidence.
Worldwide COVID-19 Panic
Due to Covid-19 and the rollout of 5G technologies occurring around the same time, global panic fuelled fears that the two are related. This is because of the severity of the coronavirus and mistrust of 5G technologies. People falsely claimed that 5G was used to spread coronaviruses through microchips implanted during vaccinations. This is factually incorrect: it is not possible for viruses to spread through electromagnetic waves (used by 5G), and similarly, there are many countries where coronavirus spread where there was no 5G. The impact of this was that many people did not get Covid vaccines, thus increasing the impact of Covid severely.
