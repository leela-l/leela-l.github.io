Quantum Fields

Now Quantum fields, they are not some secret energy fountains we can tap into, they are just very, complicated…

They form the backbone of a framework called Quantum Field Theory (QFT)...

Firstly, the entire crux of QFT is that like many physical theories, it is a plainly abstract concept...

Fields themselves are not literal and tangible...

In standard Quantum mechanics, the wavefunction is the ‘principle object’...

Instead fields are incorporated in the quantum framework...

But some particles would naturally arise out of this framework, never having been seen before in experimental settings. For example, the negative energy solutions found by Dirac, predicted the notion of Anti-Electrons or Positrons.
